## A real estate website that belongs to your agency

**[EstateSpace](https://estatespace.mk)** is a real estate website SaaS product developed by **[PROCESS IN](https://processin.mk)**. Its purpose is to give agencies a distinct, branded digital presence for presenting properties and connecting with interested buyers, renters and property owners.

A property listing is not just a collection of fields. Good presentation combines property information, media, context, agency credibility and a clear inquiry journey. EstateSpace brings those concerns into a dedicated real estate website experience.

## From agency identity to property inquiry

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **01 — Define the agency brand** | Establish the agency identity, contact context and website positioning |
| **02 — Prepare the website** | Organize navigation, content and branded presentation |
| **03 — Build the property portfolio** | Structure listings and descriptive property information |
| **04 — Present each property** | Help visitors evaluate listings with relevant details and media |
| **05 — Make contact clear** | Guide interested visitors toward an inquiry or agency conversation |
| **06 — Maintain the portfolio** | Keep the digital presentation current as properties change |
