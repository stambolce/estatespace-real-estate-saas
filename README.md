## Real estate websites with their own identity

**EstateSpace** is a white-label, multi-tenant SaaS platform for real estate websites. It is positioned around helping real estate agencies present properties under their own brands instead of relying only on shared listing portals.

The public product registry identifies EstateSpace as a **white-label multi-tenant real estate website SaaS**. This showcase describes its product positioning and intended experience; it does **not** certify that every capability below is implemented or commercially available.

### Product focus

| Area | Product direction |
|---|---|
| **Agency-branded websites** | An online presence under each agency's identity |
| **Property presentation** | Structured pages for presenting real estate listings |
| **Multi-tenant platform** | Separation of agency-specific websites and content |
| **Responsive experience** | A property browsing experience across desktop and mobile |
| **Lead generation** | Inquiry-oriented journeys for prospective buyers and renters |
| **Operational growth** | A foundation for scalable website management |

> Product directions are not a verified feature-completion checklist. Ask PROCESS IN for the current release scope and availability.

## Who it is for

Real estate agencies seeking professional branded websites, property businesses presenting their listings, and teams evaluating white-label real estate solutions.

## Product principles

**Brand ownership.** The agency's identity should be central to the visitor experience.

**Tenant separation.** Each business should have a distinct space within the platform.

**Clarity and discoverability.** Property information should be easy to navigate, understand and find.

**Practical workflows.** Product design should prioritize agency and visitor needs rather than complexity for its own sake.
